## Interactive statistical tools

### Two-sample and paired t-tests

Upload an Excel or CSV dataset, select two numeric columns, choose a two-sample or paired t-test, and view the standard R statistical output.

[Open STAT 351 t-Test Calculator]({{ '/teaching/stat351/ttest/' | relative_url }})

The app runs locally in your browser using Shinylive. It does not provide interpretations or select a statistical test for you.

### Simple linear regression

Upload a CSV or Excel (.xlsx) dataset, choose x and y, and select separate levels for confidence and prediction intervals. View R's linear-model output, coefficient confidence intervals, mean-response confidence intervals, prediction intervals, and diagnostic plots.

[Open STAT 351 Regression Calculator]({{ '/teaching/stat351/regression/' | relative_url }})
